# ESP-RotaryCell Photo Guide

These photographs will be added to the project documentation after you return them in a ZIP. Use ordinary high-resolution JPEG or PNG files. Good lighting and clear focus matter more than studio presentation.

## Required photographs

### 1. Finished phone — hero image

A clean three-quarter view of the assembled Model 500 with the handset in place. Use a plain background and avoid visual clutter. This will be the main README image.

Suggested filename: `01_finished_phone_hero.jpg`

### 2. Finished phone — straight front view

Show the full telephone, dial, handset, and body.

Suggested filename: `02_finished_phone_front.jpg`

### 3. Interior overview

Open the telephone and photograph the entire interior from directly above. All major boards and wire routes should be visible.

Suggested filename: `03_interior_overview.jpg`

### 4. LilyGO installation

A close-up showing the LilyGO board, antenna connection, battery connection, USB access, and mounting method. Labels should be readable where possible.

Suggested filename: `04_lilygo_installation.jpg`

### 5. AG1171 installation

A close-up of the AG1171, its power wiring, TIP/RING wiring, and the nearby bypass capacitors.

Suggested filename: `05_ag1171_installation.jpg`

### 6. Transformer audio board — component side

Show both transformers, VR1, VR2, capacitors, connectors, and wire labels.

Suggested filename: `06_audio_board_component_side.jpg`

### 7. Transformer audio board — solder side

Show the perfboard wiring clearly enough to inspect construction.

Suggested filename: `07_audio_board_solder_side.jpg`

### 8. Model 500 network-block connections

A close-up showing where AG1171 TIP and RING connect to the telephone network block.

Suggested filename: `08_network_block_connections.jpg`

### 9. Control wiring

Show the AG1171 F/R, RM, and SHK control connections and their route to the LilyGO/ESP32.

Suggested filename: `09_control_wiring.jpg`

### 10. Battery and power arrangement

Show the cell, holder or mounting method, power switch relationship, and charging/programming access.

Suggested filename: `10_battery_and_power.jpg`

## Helpful optional photographs

- Underside of the rotary dial and pulse contacts
- Hookswitch mechanism and wiring
- Mechanical bell and its wiring
- Cellular antenna placement
- USB-C access with the case assembled or opened
- Any drilled holes, brackets, or reversible mounting modifications
- The phone during an incoming call
- Serial monitor showing startup and network registration
- Serial monitor showing decoded rotary digits and the final `ATD` command

## How to take them

- Use diffuse room light or daylight; avoid a harsh flash directly on boards.
- Hold the camera parallel to the board for documentation close-ups.
- Tap to focus on the wiring or component labels.
- Take one wider image and one closer image if a connection is difficult to show.
- Do not resize or compress the originals before zipping them.

## ZIP preparation

Place the photos directly in one ZIP file; no folder structure is required. Use the suggested filenames when practical. Include a short text file only if a photo needs explanation.
